# Brief

**Question:** Which note-taking app should a small team of 5 people choose?

**Products to compare:**
1. Notion
2. Obsidian
3. Microsoft OneNote

**Reader:** A team lead who has 5 minutes and wants a clear recommendation.

**Compare on:** price for 5 users, collaboration, offline use, learning curve, AI features.
