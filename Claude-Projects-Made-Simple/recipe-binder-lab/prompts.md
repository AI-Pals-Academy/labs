# Prompts – Claude Projects Made Simple (Workbook 2)

Every "Type this" prompt from the workbook, in order. Copy, paste, and change anything in [square brackets].

## Practice 1 – Create your binder
Goal box ("What do you want to work on?"):
> Build a family recipe binder: one doc per dish, with servings, ingredients, steps and family notes.

## Practice 2 – Write the rules on the cover
Paste the rules from rules-template.md. To write your own rules with Claude (main chat):
> Suggest project instructions for this binder. Ask me 3 questions first, then give me rules I can paste.

## Practice 3 – Start your first thread
Main chat:
> Start a tacos recipe.

Tacos thread:
> Street-style chicken tacos, Mom's recipe from memory, serves 4. Mom warms the tortillas over an open flame. Use your best estimates for the amounts and mark them as estimates.

## Practice 4 – One request, many threads
Main chat:
> Add recipes for chili, a Greek salad and tomato soup. Use standard recipes for 4 people and mark the amounts as estimates.

Main chat (duplicate check):
> Start a new taco recipe.

## Practice 5 – Files in, artifacts out
Upload moms-tacos-card.jpg and dads-banana-bread-card.jpg (Context tab → + next to Files → Upload files).

Main chat:
> I added two handwritten recipe cards to Files. Add each one to the binder.

Tacos thread:
> Yes, replace the estimates with Mom's real amounts and add a line to Changes.

## Practice 6 – Keep your binder tidy
Main chat:
> Resolve the Chili thread.

## Practice 7 – Reach outside the binder
First connect Google Drive: your name → Settings → Connectors.

Main chat:
> Save the shopping list from this week's meal plan as a Google Sheet in my Drive, called "Shopping List – Week of <date>".

Main chat:
> Use the PDF skill to make a printable recipe booklet of all my recipe docs, with a contents page.

## Practice 8 – Put it on autopilot
Main chat:
> Set up a scheduled task: every Sunday at 9 AM, make a doc called "Meal Plan – Week of <date>" with breakfast, lunch and dinner for Monday to Sunday. Use recipes from this binder first and link to their docs. Where the binder doesn't have enough recipes, suggest simple dishes and mark them "New idea, not in binder yet." End with one shopping list grouped by produce, meat, dairy and pantry. Then run it once now so I can see it.

## Practice 9 – Design your own project
A normal Claude chat, or your new project's main chat:
> I want to set up a Claude project for [your work]. Before we build anything, interview me: ask 5 questions, one at a time. Then suggest a project name, a one-line goal, how to split the work into threads (and how to name them), what files to add, what artifacts I'll want, and project instructions I can paste.

## Useful any time (main chat)
> Give me a status update on every thread.

> Let's work on [item] again.

> Remember that [fact or preference].

> Propose threads and wait for my go-ahead before starting them.

> Run at most two threads at a time.
